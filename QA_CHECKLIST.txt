LumoLingo Working App QA Checklist

1. Open index.html locally or through GitHub Pages.
2. Confirm Home World opens and all menu buttons route correctly.
3. Open Sound Island:
   - Listen button speaks.
   - Picture cards select.
   - Check Answer gives feedback and stars.
   - Record Voice opens recording modal.
   - Next Sound changes task.
4. Open Word Forest:
   - Word listen button works.
   - Picture selection works.
   - Check and Next Word work.
5. Open Story Lake:
   - Read Aloud works.
   - Choice buttons give feedback and stars.
   - Next Story works.
6. Open Song Mountain:
   - Sing with Lumo reads lyrics.
   - Beat tap counts to 8 and awards stars.
   - Next Song works.
7. Open Speak Garden:
   - Model voice works.
   - Microphone modal opens.
   - Save Practice Attempt works even without microphone.
   - Next Sentence works.
8. Open Progress Stars:
   - Stars, badges, and activity evidence appear.
   - Download Report works.
9. Open Parent Corner:
   - Co-play cards toggle done/not done.
   - Parent notes save.
   - Download Plan works.
10. Open Teacher Studio:
   - Assign Activity creates active assignment.
   - Export CSV downloads.
   - Recordings appear after saving audio.
11. Open Admin Studio:
   - PIN unlock works.
   - Settings save.
   - Add Word Forest card works.
   - Add Speak Garden sentence works.
   - Export/Import backup works.
   - Reset Demo works.
12. Refresh the browser:
   - Progress and settings remain saved.
